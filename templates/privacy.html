Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracker, so almost everything you put into it is information about your own money. This policy explains what we collect, why we hold it, and what we never do with it.

1. Data We Collect

Account details. When you register we collect your name, email address, and a password. The password is never stored as you typed it — only a one-way hash is kept, which cannot be turned back into the original.

Expense records. Everything you log yourself: the amount, category, date, and any description you add. This is the core of the service and is entirely yours.

Basic technical data. Standard server logs such as request times and error details, kept so the service can be kept running and debugged.

We do not ask for and do not want your bank credentials, card numbers, or account numbers. Spendly has no connection to your bank and cannot see your real transactions — you enter expenses manually.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the service you signed up for: to authenticate you, to show your expense history, and to produce the category breakdowns, monthly summaries, and date-range filters the app is built around.

We may use aggregated, anonymised figures — for example, the total number of accounts — to understand how the service is being used. These figures cannot be traced back to any individual.

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use your expense records to build advertising or credit profiles. We will only disclose data where we are legally required to do so.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database and retained for as long as your account is active.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including password hashing and restricted access to the database. No system is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against every possible breach.

You can delete individual expense records at any time, or delete your account along with all data associated with it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so keep your own copies of anything you want to retain.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly deliberately relies on very few outside services. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit; that request is handled under Google's own privacy policy. No expense data is involved.

We do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, or tracking pixels, and we do not place cookies for any purpose beyond keeping you signed in.

If we introduce a new third-party service in future that handles personal data, we will update this policy before doing so.

5. Your Rights

You can view and correct your account details and expense records from within the app at any time, and you can withdraw from the service by deleting your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have a right to request a copy of your data or object to certain processing.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want a copy of your data, or would like something corrected or deleted, please get in touch through the contact details published on the Spendly website. We aim to respond to privacy requests promptly.

This policy may be updated from time to time; the “last updated” date above always reflects the current version. See our Terms and Conditions for the rules covering use of the service.
